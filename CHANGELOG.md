# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-19)

- Dark theme. The app follows the system setting on start, and the ⋮ menu switches it.
  Charts are drawn for both themes and swap instantly, without waiting for a rerun.
- The ⋮ menu is back for viewers (it was hidden by mistake in 0.1.0); the Deploy button stays
  hidden.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-19)

First release.

- Compare and rank aligned discontinuous-fiber composites, with a written explanation per
  factor and the matrix threshold check.
- Continuous fibers, discontinuous fibers, orientation and particle pages.
- Step-by-step formula panels and an A4 PDF report, in English and Turkish.
